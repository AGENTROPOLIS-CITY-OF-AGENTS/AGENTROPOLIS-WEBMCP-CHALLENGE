Do not remove upstream attribution from source derived from ABYSSAL.

## Integration rule

Do not invent or assume ABYSSAL public methods. The terminal integration pass must map the adapter to verified upstream classes/functions. The provider membrane exists specifically to prevent upstream implementation details from leaking into the Intelligence Grid contract.
